HOW TO RUN ONE
  Paste the whole of one paste file into the business Pricing page and press
  Save. Or send it to the API directly:

    POST http://localhost:8787/api/v1/business
    { "action": "submit", "businessUid": "demo-rw-1", "trade": "retaining_wall",
      "text": "<paste here>" }

  The slug is retaining_wall, with an UNDERSCORE. Sending "retaining-wall" or
  omitting `trade` writes to the wrong document - it defaults to fencing.

  Use a DIFFERENT businessUid per case so they do not overwrite each other.

  Approval is not the end. Nothing is quotable to a customer until the business
  presses Confirm on its own screen:

    { "action": "confirm", "businessUid": "demo-rw-1", "trade": "retaining_wall" }

WHAT MAKES THIS TRADE DIFFERENT, AND WHAT TO POINT OUT WHILE DEMOING
  1. THE SAME WALL IS PRICED TWICE. A builder either supplies the sleepers or
     installs the customer's own, and publishes a complete per-metre rate for
     each. Timber sleepers are $145 a metre one way and $285 the other. Case 1
     below stores nine rates across two tables, not nine rates in one.
  2. A RATE WITH NO SUPPLY MODEL IS UNUSABLE. "$185 per metre" is a bargain
     with the materials included and ordinary without, and nothing in the
     number says which. It is reported rather than guessed at - guessing is
     worth $140 a metre in whichever direction it goes wrong.
  3. HOURLY AND PER-POST WORK NEVER ENTERS A QUOTE. Excavation at $95/hr and
     post holes at $75/post are stored and shown to the customer as what is not
     included. A customer cannot say how many hours or count posts, and the AI
     is never allowed to work it out.
  4. WALLS ARE STRUCTURAL. The system asks where a business stands on
     engineering and council approval, and never states that a given wall does
     or does not need it.

WHAT CASE 2 IS TOLD TO FIX
  - Say whether you supply the materials, install what the customer buys, or
    both - and give a rate per metre for each one you do.
  - Say what heights your rates cover - either a price per height band, or one
    line saying a single rate covers every height you build.
  - Add the smallest job you will take on and what you charge for it.
  - Say where you work out of, how far you travel, and whether your prices
    include GST.
  - Give one firm price per metre for each wall system - some of what you sent
    is a range or a "call us".

  Note what is NOT in that list: drainage, removal, excavation and engineering
  are all SATISFIED. "Priced with the job", "priced on inspection" and "quoted
  separately once we've seen the site" are complete answers. The rule is that
  every item must be STATED, not that every item must carry a price - otherwise
  a specialist who genuinely does not offer something could never publish.
