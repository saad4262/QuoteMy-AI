================================================================================
QuoteMy AI — BUSINESS ONBOARDING TEST PACK
For showing the client what the system does. 12 cases, worst to best.
================================================================================

Every outcome written below was produced by running that exact text through the
live system (gpt-5.6-terra). Nothing here is described from expectation.

HOW TO RUN ONE
  Paste the text between the ----- lines into the business Pricing page and
  press Save. Or send it to the API directly:

    POST http://localhost:8787/api/v1/business
    { "action": "submit", "businessUid": "demo-1", "trade": "fencing",
      "text": "<paste here>" }

  Use a DIFFERENT businessUid for each case (demo-1, demo-2, ...) so they do
  not overwrite each other.

EVERY RESPONSE NOW CARRIES TWO LISTS
  "What needs fixing"  - why it was sent back. Blocks approval.
  "Worth adding"       - what would make the profile stronger. Blocks nothing,
                         and appears on APPROVED submissions too.

  Worth showing on Case 12: a complete profile gets one suggestion, not five.
  And on a four-line price list it gets four - which is the difference between
  a business being approved with a skeleton profile and being told what would
  actually win them work.

THREE THINGS TO POINT OUT WHILE DEMOING
  1. A rejection is not an error. The business did nothing wrong by sending an
     incomplete list, so it comes back as a normal result with a list of jobs.
  2. Every number stored has to be traceable to a sentence the business
     actually wrote. Anything the AI cannot point at is dropped and reported.
  3. Nothing goes live until the business presses Confirm. Approval is the
     machine's opinion; going live is the business's decision.

What it proves: the same thing priced two different ways is caught and queried,
not silently resolved.
Result: rejected, with the exact contradiction quoted back.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Minimum charge is $850.

TREATED PINE PALING
1.5m high — $79 per metre
1.8m high — $85 per metre
1.8m high — $95 per metre

COLORBOND
1.8m high — $110 per metre

REMOVAL OF EXISTING FENCE
$180 per metre to pull down and cart away an old timber fence.
$18 per metre for an old timber fence.

GATES
Single pedestrian gate, timber — $480
Double / driveway gate, timber — $1,340
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The actual response:

  1. Replace the two different 1.8m treated-pine rates with the one set price
     you actually charge.
       e.g. Treated pine paling 1.8m — $85 per metre (your figure)
  2. Confirm the old timber-fence removal rate, as it says both $180 and $18
     per metre.
       e.g. Old timber fence removal — $18 per metre (your figure)

This is the case worth dwelling on. A human reading 80 lines of price list would
very likely miss both. And if either went through, a customer gets quoted a
number the business never meant.

Minimum charge is $800.

TREATED PINE
1.8m high - $90 per metre

COLORBOND
1.8m high - $118 per metre

Bamboo screen fencing - 1.8m at $74 per metre.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THIS IS THE ONE TO SHOW THE CLIENT if they only watch one thing.

Business A wrote "BAMBOO SCREENING". Business B wrote "bamboo screen fencing".

stop matching.

================================================================================
AFTER AN APPROVAL — THE PART THAT MATTERS MOST
================================================================================

Run Case 12, then press Confirm on the business screen.

Before Confirm, the profile is marked "verified" — checked by the system, and
NOT visible to customers. Confirm is what makes it live.

Tell the client plainly why: the AI can misread a price list. If "$85" is ever
read as "$35", Confirm is the moment a human catches it, before a customer is
quoted a number the business would lose money honouring.


================================================================================
WHAT TO SAY IF THE CLIENT ASKS "HOW DO WE KNOW THE NUMBERS ARE RIGHT?"
================================================================================

Three checks, and every stored number passes all three:

  1. SOURCE SENTENCE. The AI must return the exact sentence each number came
     from, and that sentence has to actually appear in what the business wrote.
     If it does not, the number was invented and is dropped — and the business
     is told which one and why.

  2. PLAUSIBILITY. A rate over $2,000 per metre, a height outside 0.3-4m, a
     travel radius over 500km are refused even when a real-looking sentence is
     attached. This caught a genuine case of an $8,500/m rate whose source
     sentence did exist in the text.

  3. THE AI NEVER DOES ARITHMETIC. "$85/m plus $15/m for removal" is stored as
     a rate of 85 and a removal of 15 — never added into 100. On a 30 metre job
     that difference is $450 versus $2,550.

And if nothing survives those checks, the profile is stored as "unverified"
rather than approved. An empty price list is never labelled as checked.
