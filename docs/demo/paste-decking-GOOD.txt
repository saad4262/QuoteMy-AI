ENGINEERING AND BUILDING PERMITS
Whether a deck needs a building permit depends on its height, its position, how close it is to a
boundary and whether it is attached to the house. We check it on every job rather than assuming, and
we never tell a customer a permit is unnecessary without confirming it for their site.
We arrange engineering from $890 and the building permit application from $650. Council and surveyor
fees are the customer's. Engineering and permits are excluded unless the written quotation includes
them.
